James C. Gee, Ph.D., is Professor of Radiologic Science in Radiology at the Perelman School of Medicine and Director of the Penn Image Computing and Science Laboratory. Jim's major area of interest is biomedical image analysis and computing, with active research in all of the quantitative methods represented, including segmentation, registration, morphometry and shape statistics, as applied to a variety of organ systems and all of the major and emerging modalities in biological/biomaterials imaging and in vivo medical imaging.

- Professor of Radiologic Science in Radiology, Perelman School of Medicine
- Director, Penn Image Computing and Science Laboratory, Department of Radiology
- Co-Director, Translational Bio-Imaging Center, Institute for Translational Medicine and Therapeutics
- Director, MSE in Data Science Online Program, School of Engineering and Applied Science
- Director, Interfaces Program in Biomedical Imaging and Informational Sciences
- Faculty Affiliate, General Robotics, Automation, Sensing and Perception (GRASP) Laboratory

Member of the Bioengineering and Computer and Information Science graduate groups.
