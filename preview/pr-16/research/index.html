Research

PICSL develops computational methods for biomedical image analysis, including segmentation, registration, morphometry and shape analysis, and applies them across organ systems and imaging modalities.

Projects

Sharper medial temporal lobe segmentation from isotropic training

An implicit neural representation builds an isotropic training atlas from anisotropic T2-weighted MRI, giving MTL subregion measures with stronger effect sizes and better test-retest stability. Published in NeuroImage.

Postmortem MRI links deep brain atrophy to specific neurodegenerative pathologies

Postmortem MRI and neuropathology from 132 brain donors reveal disease- and region-specific links between subcortical and limbic atrophy and tau, α-synuclein and TDP-43. Published in Alzheimer’s & Dementia.

FireANTs: fast, training-free image registration on the GPU

A training-free, GPU-accelerated registration algorithm from the Gee lab that is two orders of magnitude faster than ANTs on a GPU and robust across modalities, species and organs. Published in Nature Communications.
