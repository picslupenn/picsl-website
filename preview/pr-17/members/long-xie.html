Research Overview I am a research scientist at the University of Pennsylvania, under the direction of Prof. Paul Yushkevich and Prof. David Wolk. My current research interest is in developing novel imaging biomarkers as well as improving the current ones for early diagnosis and monitoring of Alzheimer’s Disease (AD). More Info: Google Scholar / LinkedIn / Research Gate Projects

  • Multi-Template Analysis of Human Perirhinal Cortex (PRC) in Brain MRI: Explicitly Accounting for Anatomical Variability
  • Accounting for the Confound of Meninges in Segmenting Entorhinal and Perirhinal Cortices in T1-weighted MRI
  • Arterial Spin Labeling (ASL) Data Cleaning and Analysis
