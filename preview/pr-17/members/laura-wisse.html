Laura Wisse Alumni

Research Overview My main interests are imaging of the medial temporal lobe in aging and diseases such as Alzheimer disease and Major Depressive Disorder. I am involved in the development of new tools for assessing medial temporal lobe structures, but I am also interested in investigating how these medial temporal lobe structures relate to aging, disease and cognitive functions, such as memory. I am part of the Hippocampus Gang, led by Paul Yushkevich and David Wolk. Education 2010: MSc in Psychology at Leiden University, The Netherlands 2014: MSc in Epidemiology at Utrecht University, The Netherlands 2014: PhD in Neuroscience at Utrecht University, The Netherlands Grants
